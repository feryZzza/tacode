\section{Metric Identity and Clean-Adjusted Contrast}

For the evaluated gate, $u_n^{g}=g_nu_n^0$ with $g_n\in[0,1]$. Hence
\[
\mathbb{I}[u_n^{g}\tau_n^\star<0]
=\mathbb{I}[u_n^0\tau_n^\star<0]\mathbb{I}[g_n>0],
\]
so the wrong-direction fraction cannot increase and changes only at exact
shutdowns. The archived clean/fault wrong-direction non-increase checks are
therefore identities, not active constraints. The main paper accordingly makes
the continuously responsive $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ primary and retains the count
only as a hard-shutdown diagnostic.

Within each seed and task split, we subtract the clean gate change from every
fault-scenario change and then macro-average the nine faults. The raw fault
change, matched clean change, and clean-adjusted change are respectively
$-0.736\pm0.067$, $-0.397\pm0.043$, and $-0.339\pm0.098$ percentage points for
the wrong-direction fraction. For $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ they are
$-0.0114\pm0.0020$, $-0.0044\pm0.0009$, and $-0.0070\pm0.0015$
$(\mathrm{Nm/kg})^2\mathrm{s}$. These standard deviations are across the three
training seeds. This is a scenario-aggregate matched contrast, not a
window-level paired confidence interval.

\section{Reselection, Matched Controls, and Transients}

\paragraph{Constrained reselection.}
The revised rule minimizes validation $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ over $216$ candidates
per seed, formed by crossing the softness grid with causal rate limits
$\lambda^{\downarrow},\lambda^{\uparrow}\in\{0,0.001,0.0025,0.005,0.02,0.05\}$,
subject to six clean-side constraints: aligned-command retention,
capped-overlap retention, tracking RMSE, full-shutdown fraction, mean absolute
torque rate, and false shutdowns per minute. All six hold in every seed. At the
selected points the validation clean values are $X_{\mathrm{opp}}=0.1003$,
tracking RMSE $0.0456$~Nm/kg, capped-overlap retention $0.631$, full-shutdown
fraction $0.0028$, $0.510$ shutdowns per minute, and mean absolute torque rate
$0.258$~Nm\,kg$^{-1}$\,s$^{-1}$; validation fault-side $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ is
$0.1129$. Seed 7 selects $(\gamma,\lambda^{\downarrow},\lambda^{\uparrow})=
(0.5,0.0025,0.005)$ and seeds 13 and 23 select $(4.0,0.02,0.02)$, so the
deadband varies over $0.263$--$0.339$ and the rate limits are not identical
across seeds.

\paragraph{Nine-gate comparison and randomization.}
The matched comparison spans $280$ rows per seed over nine gates plus
leave-one-out variants, with $1000$-draw randomization in $120$ cells. On ID
faults, fault-side $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ is $0.0479$ with all six channels,
$0.0479$ without the fault logit (bit-identical), $0.0479$ without aleatoric,
$0.0479$ without epistemic, $0.0479$ without staleness, $0.0481$ without
forecast, $0.0482$ for the validation-selected subset, $0.0482$ for
reconstruction only, $0.0493$ without reconstruction, $0.0534$ for the oracle
error gate, $0.0565$ for staleness only and for fault only (both exactly equal
to ungated, $0.0565$), and $0.0223$ for a hard threshold at $170$ shutdowns per
minute. Random attenuation attains percentile $\geq 0.5$ in $11$ cells; the
worst is $\mathrm{test\_id}/\mathrm{packet\_loss}$ at percentile $1.000$
($0.04101$ for the gate versus $0.04084$ for the permutation), whereas the
strongest cell against random shutdown reaches only $0.272$.

\paragraph{Rate-limit and hysteresis grid.}
The dynamics sweep contains $468$ rows per seed. Relative to ungated ID faults
($X_{\mathrm{opp}}=0.0620$, torque rate $0.267$), $36$ rate-limited settings
lower $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ without raising torque rate. Representative points:
$(\lambda^{\downarrow},\lambda^{\uparrow})=(0,0.001)$ gives $0.0278$ at torque
rate $0.096$ and clean retention $0.501$; $(0.05,0.001)$ gives $0.0322$ at
$0.115$ and $0.625$; $(0.02,0.001)$ gives $0.0371$ at $0.138$, clean retention
$0.710$, and $1.32$ clean shutdowns per minute. Hysteresis gives $0.0083$ at
clean retention $0.421$ with a $2360$~ms $95$th-percentile closure.

\paragraph{Transients.}
The transient protocol contributes $1608$ rows across onset fractions, six
durations, recovery and no-recovery conditions, and a clean-pause negative
control. Rows without a finite duration are the no-recovery condition, in which
the fault persists to the window end; their onset latency is defined but
recovery latency is necessarily absent. Miss rates and onset-to-$g<0.5$ delays
are $0.945/5.2$~ms, $0.933/12.9$~ms, $0.898/36.6$~ms, $0.814/102.4$~ms,
$0.767/266.4$~ms, and $0.551/612.4$~ms for $25$, $50$, $100$, $250$, $500$~ms
and persistent faults. False shutdowns are $18.9$--$19.2$ per minute in all
fault conditions and $18.9$ in the clean-pause control, with $54.3$ alarm
transitions per minute in the control.

\paragraph{Paired analysis and reverse ablation.}
The paired analysis matches windows on (participant, trial, window start),
yielding $13{,}041$ pairs with zero unmatched windows dropped over $51$ trials
and $216$ summary cells. Because only two participants are held out, the
participant-level bootstrap admits three distinct resampled means, so all $216$
cells are flagged underpowered; $48$ of $54$ $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ cells have
intervals excluding zero and $52$ have negative point estimates, and $66$ of
$72$ across-seed contrasts are sign-consistent. The reverse ablation reports
eight cells at three seeds each: the seven required leave-one-component-out
configurations plus an all-channel reference. The Pareto comparison restricts
the fault-side mean to the operator set shared by all cells, since the trained
and eval-side cells were run over different operator lists.

\section{Reproduction Notes}

The submission package contains the official AAAI reproducibility checklist
and a separate anonymous reproduction note. It also includes the scripts that
construct the aggregate tables and figures from archived per-run JSON reports.
The legacy JSON field names \texttt{wrong\_energy} and
\texttt{mean\_abs\_jerk} are compatibility aliases: the corresponding
quantities are the opposition-weighted torque-product integral in
$(\mathrm{Nm/kg})^2\mathrm{s}$ and mean absolute torque rate in
Nm\,kg$^{-1}$\,s$^{-1}$, respectively.

Main training uses batch size 64, 20 epochs, AdamW with learning rate
$10^{-3}$ and weight decay $10^{-4}$, gradient-norm clipping at 5, 768-sample
windows at stride 384, at most four windows per trial, and no learning-rate
schedule or early stopping. With the configured negative
\texttt{clean\_sample\_prob}, clean and each of the six training faults are
sampled uniformly. The checkpoint minimizes clean-validation
$\mathrm{RMSE}+0.02\,\mathrm{coverage\ ECE}$ over epochs.

All runs use a single NVIDIA A100 80GB PCIe GPU (driver 570.211.01) on an
x86-64 host with 503 GB of system memory under Ubuntu 22.04.5 LTS (Linux
6.2.1), with Python 3.12 and PyTorch 2.11.0 built against CUDA 12.8. Training
one seed takes $30$--$50$ GPU-minutes depending on the enabled heads, and seeds
run concurrently on separate devices; the gate reselection, matched-control,
transient, paired, and reverse-ablation analyses reuse archived checkpoints and
are evaluation-only.

The staleness statistic uses threshold $10^{-3}$ and a 25-sample trailing
window; coherence uses a 32-sample trailing window and clamps each rolling
variance below at $10^{-8}$. The main suite uses 10 MC-dropout passes.
Hip/knee moment-to-torque scales are $(0.20,0.15)$, delays are $(10,0)$ samples,
the low-pass cutoff is 6 Hz, and saturation is 0.45 Nm/kg. Coverage ECE uses
nominal levels 0.50, 0.68, 0.90, and 0.95. $X_{\mathrm{opp}}$ is summed over
the evaluated samples and is not normalized by recording duration; all paired
comparisons use identical samples. Each LOSO fold uses training seed 7 and the
preceding participant as validation, so the 15 fitted training sets overlap.
